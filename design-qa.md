# Schedule setup design QA

Final result: passed

The setup page follows the selected compact dashboard direction, using four panels with border-only elevation: Competition and Availability on the left, Courts and Game rules on the right. A sticky footer shows the summary and actions. The league and season appear once beneath the title, field labels fit on one line at desktop width, selected playing days use the primary color, and less-used availability settings are under More options.

Desktop (1280 px) and mobile (390 px) captures were reviewed. On mobile, the panels stack in reading order and the footer shows only the actions. A test preview produced 34 games.

Intentional differences from the mockup: best-of-three playoff series and per-venue court counts are omitted because the product does not support them.
